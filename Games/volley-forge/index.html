Field manual

How to play

  1. Choose a starter from your owned balls before each run, including purchased legendary balls.
  2. Select any glowing ball in your arsenal.
  3. Drag and release above the cannon to aim and fire.
  4. Exhaust every ball. Surviving blocks then descend one row.
  5. Break every block before any of them crosses the molten danger line.
  6. Level up from destroyed blocks and choose one of three upgrades.

Keyboard: 1–4 select · arrows aim · Space fires · R recalls after 12s · P pauses

Forgeblade: drag its exact target reticle and release, or use all four arrow keys and Space.
